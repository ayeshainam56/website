Immigration law in the U.S. is complex, but understanding the essentials can make a life-changing difference.  
At **Malik & Associates LLC**, we help clients navigate these challenges every day. Here are the **five most important things you need to know in 2025**:

## 1. Visa Processing Times Are Changing Fast

Government processing times are shifting quickly (as of July 2025):

- **Family visas:** 8–16 months (down from 12–24 months in 2024)  
- **Work visas:** 3–9 months depending on category  
- **Citizenship applications:** 10–14 months  

👉 You can check your case status at [USCIS Case Status](https://egov.uscis.gov/casestatus/).

---

## 2. The 90-Day Rule Is Stricter Than Ever

The rule now applies not only to tourist visas but also **student visas**:  
- Filing for a green card within 90 days of entering may raise a presumption of visa fraud.  

📌 Real example from our firm:  
One of our clients applied for adjustment of status 85 days after entering on a B-2 visa and received a **Notice of Intent to Deny**. With our intervention, we were able to present a strong case.

---

## 3. Essential Documents Checklist

Before every USCIS appointment, ensure you bring:  

1. Original passport + copies  
2. I-94 arrival record ([print here](https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov))  
3. Certified translations of foreign documents  
4. Two recent 2x2 passport photos (white background)  
5. Money order for fees (to avoid delays from personal checks)  

---

## 4. Common Reasons for Application Denials

Based on our extensive case files:  

1. **Incomplete forms** — especially I-485  
2. **Missed deadlines** — USCIS does not send reminders  
3. **Criminal history** — even decades-old charges can matter  
4. **Public charge concerns** — always provide proof of income/assets
